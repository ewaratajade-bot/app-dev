# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Introduction
My favorite series is **Stranger Things**. It is a science fiction, horror, and mystery series created by the Duffer Brothers.

## About the Series
Stranger Things is about a group of friends who discover strange events happening in their town called Hawkins. They experience mysterious creatures, secret experiments, and adventures in another dimension called the Upside Down.

## Main Characters
Some of my favorite characters are:

- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers
- Max Mayfield

## Why I Like This Series
I like this series because:
1. It has an interesting story.
2. The characters have strong friendships.
3. It has exciting and mysterious scenes.
4. Every episode makes me curious about what will happen next.

## My Favorite Character
**Eleven** is my favorite character because she is brave, strong, and always tries to protect her friends.

## Favorite Quote
> Friends don't lie.
